Qualifying products

Map 19L refill to one refill unit. Map cartons directly, or set units per reward to 6 for individual 1.5L bottles and 12 for individual 500ml bottles. Security deposits and delivery fees must remain unmapped.

Company prices and stock

Set the price for the ERP selling unit: one refill, one bottle or one carton. Existing orders retain their recorded prices. Manage stock receipts and adjustments in ERP Inventory.

Bottle security and delivery terms

Each company sets its own terms. These are launch preparation records; they do not collect deposits or add delivery fees to checkout. Existing deposits remain unchanged.
