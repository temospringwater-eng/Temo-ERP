Questions

About TEMO AQUAFLOW

Does the ERP support multiple companies?

The central ERP is designed with separate companies and role-based access. Full Cloudflare D1 migration remains in testing.

Does the Driver App work offline?

Native offline workflows exist in QA. Final live offline-to-central-database synchronization must pass end-to-end testing before production.

Can I create a new company account today?

Public self-service signup and automated subscriptions are not yet live. Existing authorized users can use the ERP Login link.
